Stray Dogs

Stray Dogs Coffee is a dog-friendly and Christian coffee shop located in Riverside, California.

Stray Dogs is such a cute coffee shop, it's in a great location, but I would say the only con of being located in downtown Riverside is that parking can be limited and hard to find. Last time i went I got a seasonal drink, their Blueberry Crumple Matcha (admititdly minus the crumples) and I loved it.
